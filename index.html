SOS

Press and hold the button, or double-tap it, to arm. You'll get a 5-second countdown you can cancel before anything is sent.

When it fires, Safety Net asks your browser for your live location to include in the alert. If you say no, you can type your location in instead.

    Route history (opt-in)

        SOS always only shares your live location at the moment it fires,

    Chat check-in
    
      
    
        Type how you're doing. This runs entirely on your device — nothing
        you type here is sent anywhere by itself. If your message looks like
        it might describe a distress situation, Safety Net will ask before
        sending anything; it never sends an alert without your confirmation.
      
    
      
    
        ⚠️ This is a simple on-device keyword check, not a crisis service or
        a clinical tool. If you are in immediate physical danger, contact
        your local emergency number directly the moment you're able to.
