🛟 Safety Net

One-tap SOS with live location, a safe walking-route planner, a check-in timer that alerts your contact if you go quiet, and a chat-based distress check — all in one place for commutes, walks home, and unfamiliar places.

Note on "staying set up": your trusted contact is saved only in this browser's local storage — there's no account, no password, and no server behind this app, so this isn't real login/authentication. See HANDOFF.md for the honest breakdown and what a future backend would need to add for real accounts.

What do you need right now?

🆘

SOS Alert

Press-and-hold or double-tap to arm a 5s countdown, then send your live location to your trusted contact.

 🗺️

Safe Route Planner

Get 2-3 walking routes between two points with a demo safety score for each.

 📍

Route History

Opt-in, on-device tracking of your path while you walk — off by default.

 ⏱️

Check-in Timer

Set a timer before you head out. Miss it, and Safety Net alerts your contact automatically.

 💬

Chat Check-in

Type how you're doing — a flagged message asks before sending anything, never sends silently.
